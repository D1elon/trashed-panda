How to play

  1. Climb the shelves and swipe bottles off. Smash them back to back for a combo, up to x3.

  2. Drink the puddles. The drunker you get, the more every point is worth, up to x3.

  3. Don’t hit 100% buzz. You’ll black out for three seconds, and drunk climbing gets slippery.

  4. Smash the Holiday Reserve. Two red bottles hide on the shelves. Each one adds five seconds.

Keyboard

  • LeftRight or AD Move
  • UpDown or WS Climb (hold to keep climbing)
  • Space or E Swipe / Drink
  • P or Esc Pause

Touch

  • D-pad: move and climb
  • Paw button: swipe on a shelf, drink on the floor

    The true story

    Thanksgiving weekend, 2025. Ashland, Virginia.

    Sometime overnight, a raccoon dropped through a ceiling tile into the local ABC store. It knocked bottles off the shelves, sampled whatever hit the floor, and set off the alarm doing it.

    The next morning, Hanover County Animal Protection found the culprit passed out on the bathroom floor. They let it sleep it off and released it back into the wild with, as the shelter put it, zero signs of injury, other than maybe a hangover and poor life choices.
