How to play

  1. Climb the shelves and swipe bottles off. Smash them back to back for a combo, up to x3.

  2. Drink the puddles. The drunker you get, the more every point is worth, up to x3.

  3. Don’t hit 100% buzz. You’ll black out for three seconds, and drunk climbing gets slippery.

  4. Smash the Holiday Reserve. Two red bottles hide on the shelves. Each one adds five seconds.

Keyboard

  • LeftRight or AD Move
  • UpDown or WS Climb (hold to keep climbing)
  • Space or E Swipe / Drink
  • P or Esc Pause · M Mute · F Full screen

Touch

  • Left thumb: move and climb. Anywhere around the pad works.
  • Right thumb: the paw swipes on a shelf and drinks on the floor.
